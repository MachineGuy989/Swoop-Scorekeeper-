SWOOP Scorekeeper Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

SWOOP Scorekeeper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores

To keep score, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • The player names you type in.
  • The scores and game settings you enter.
  • Each player's finished-game record (games played, won, and lost, and last score), shown in Player History.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. You can erase game data at any time with New Game and Clear History, and deleting the app removes everything it stored.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or other personal information.
  • No network connection is needed or used. The app works fully offline.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

The website version

A web version of the scorekeeper is available at machineguy989.github.io/Swoop-Scorekeeper-. It works the same way: your game data is saved only in your own browser's local storage and is never sent to a server.

Two third parties are involved when you open the website:

  • Google Fonts supplies the page's typefaces, so your browser contacts Google to download them. Google receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser type. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log visits as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The iPhone and iPad app does not contact either of these.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
